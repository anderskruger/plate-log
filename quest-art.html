Quest exercise sprites

I can't paint pictures or make photo-style images, but I can draw pixel art with code. Every figure on this page is drawn live from one small stick-figure rig, at 24 to 48 pixels, then scaled up. The same poses give four different looks, and they move.

What this can do

  • All 19 quests get a picture from the same rig: one pose pair each, a few lines of numbers.
  • No image files. The art is drawn in the app, so it works offline and adds almost nothing to the download.
  • Original art, so it's fine on the public site, like the quest sounds.
  • It can also show on the lock-screen card as a small still picture.

What it can't do

  • No painted or photo-style images. I don't have an image generator here, only code.
  • Figures are seen from the side. Moves that only read from the front, like a side bend, need a second rig view.
  • At 24 px the face is a dot. Detail grows with the pixel size, which can go anywhere from 24 to 64.
